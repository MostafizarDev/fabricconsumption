HELP & GUIDANCE

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about the platform and its garment calculation tools.

Visit Help & FAQ →
What is a fabric consumption calculator?

A fabric consumption calculator helps estimate the fabric required for a garment or order using inputs such as measurements, fabric width, GSM, quantity and wastage. The exact method depends on the selected calculator.

Which garment calculations can I perform?

You can access knit garment, knit pant, woven shirt and marker consumption tools, along with booking, trims, zipper, size ratio, fabric costing, FOB costing and unit conversion tools.

Can I download calculation reports?

PDF or spreadsheet exports are available in supported tools. Available report formats and fields may differ by calculator.

Are the results final production approvals?

No. Treat results as calculation support and estimates. Verify important values against approved patterns, markers, fabric testing, supplier information and your organization's production procedures.
