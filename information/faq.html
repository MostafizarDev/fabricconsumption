Help & FAQ

Guidance for choosing a calculator, entering information, saving work and checking your results.

Getting started

Which tool should I use? Choose the page that matches your task: Marker Consumption for marker-based calculations; Knit Garments, Knit Pant or Woven Shirt for garment consumption; Booking Sheet for order and fabric summaries; Pricing & Costing for knit price and FOB; Zipper or Trims for accessory calculations; Size Ratio for quantity distribution; and Unit Converter for unit conversions.

How can I get a reliable result?

Enter verified measurements and rates, select the correct unit and calculation method, and review all allowances, wastage, ratios and other assumptions. Compare important outputs with your approved marker, pattern, fabric test or factory method before using them.

Can I change units?

Several calculators and the Unit Converter support unit selection or conversion. Confirm the displayed unit labels before entering values; mixing centimetres, inches, metres, yards, kilograms or other units can change the result significantly.

Can I prepare or download reports?

Report and export options vary by tool. Look for the PDF or Excel report controls on the relevant calculator. Review the report and its inputs before sharing or using it as an order record.

What does Booking Sheet help me organize?

Booking Sheet brings order information, fabric rows, marker details, size ratios and fabric-wise summaries together. Check every order detail and fabric calculation before exporting or circulating the booking report.

What can I save in My Workspace?

My Workspace supports notes, formulas, links and screenshots. Notes, formulas and links are saved in browser local storage; screenshot/image records use browser IndexedDB. Use Export Backup to keep a copy before clearing browser data or moving to another device.

Will my Workspace appear on another device?

No automatic cloud synchronization or user account is provided by the current Workspace. Your saved items remain in the browser storage on the device where you created them unless you export and transfer a backup.

Why is my result different from a manual calculation?

Check units, entered dimensions, calculation method, size ratio, allowances, wastage, rates and rounding. Make sure you are comparing the same formula and assumptions. If the difference remains, send the steps and a screenshot through Contact Us.

What if a page or button does not work?

Refresh the page, check your internet connection and try the latest version of a modern browser. If the issue continues, contact us with the page name, steps to reproduce the issue, browser and device details, and a screenshot where possible.

How do I report a bug or suggest an improvement?

Open Contact Us and use the contact links. Include the tool name and a clear description. Do not include passwords, payment details or confidential buyer or factory information.
