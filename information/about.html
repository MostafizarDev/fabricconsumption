About Fabric Consumption

A practical toolkit for garment professionals to calculate fabric consumption, plan materials, prepare booking reports and review costing in one place.

What is Fabric Consumption?

Fabric Consumption is a browser-based garment calculation suite designed to bring commonly used apparel calculations into one convenient workspace. It helps users organize inputs, calculate estimates and prepare reports without moving between multiple separate tools.

Explore the calculators

  • Marker Consumption: Work with marker-based fabric consumption and review calculated results.
  • Knit Garments: Calculate knit garment consumption using garment measurements and selected calculation settings.
  • Knit Pant: Estimate pant consumption using quick calculation or component-based measurements.
  • Woven Shirt: Work through woven garment component measurements and consumption calculations.
  • Booking Sheet: Organize order information, fabric rows, marker details and size ratios, then export booking reports.
  • Pricing & Costing: Review knit price and FOB costing calculations.
  • Zipper Consumption Planner: Calculate zipper requirements using the available calculation method and inputs.
  • Trims Calculator: Work with thread, button and interlining calculations.
  • Size Ratio: Calculate and distribute quantities using size ratios, with additional planning modes.
  • Unit Converter: Convert common length, weight, fabric, GSM, area, yarn-count, temperature and production-related units.
  • My Workspace: Save notes, formulas, links and screenshots in your browser, and export a backup.

Who can use it?

The tools may be useful for garment merchandisers, fabric and production teams, print and technical teams, students, and anyone who regularly works with apparel measurements, consumption or costing. The right result always depends on accurate inputs and the user's production method.

How to get the best result

  • Choose the calculator that matches the garment or task.
  • Confirm the unit system and enter measurements carefully.
  • Check allowances, wastage, size ratios and other assumptions before calculating.
  • Review the result and report before using it in a real order or production decision.
  • Keep your own backup of important reports and Workspace items.
Our purpose: Make everyday garment calculations more organized and accessible. Fabric Consumption is a support tool; it does not replace approved patterns, markers, fabric testing, supplier confirmation or your company's production procedures.
