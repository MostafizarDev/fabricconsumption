PRIVACY & DATA

Privacy Policy

This page explains the main ways information may be handled when you use Fabric Consumption.

Last updated: 19 November 2026

1. Information you enter

The calculator pages are designed to process calculation inputs in your browser and display results. Do not enter confidential buyer, employee, supplier, financial or order information unless you are comfortable using it on your device and understand your organization's data rules.

2. My Workspace and browser storage

My Workspace saves notes, formulas and links in browser local storage, and saves screenshot/image items in the browser's IndexedDB storage. These records are associated with the browser and device you use. They are not automatically synchronized to another device through a Fabric Consumption account. Clearing site data, changing browsers or using private browsing may make locally saved items unavailable. Export a backup if you need to keep them.

3. Reports and downloaded files

When a calculator offers a report or export, the website prepares a file for download. Downloaded files are then stored and managed by your device or browser. You are responsible for protecting, sharing and deleting those files.

4. Hosting and third-party resources

The website is delivered through web hosting infrastructure. The hosting provider may process technical request information such as IP address, browser details and request time as part of delivering and securing the website. The site may also load external resources, including web fonts, icons or software libraries; those providers may receive technical connection information when your browser requests their resources. Their own privacy terms govern their services.

5. Accounts, tracking and data sharing

The current calculator interface does not provide a sign-in account or cloud-sync feature. This statement is about the features visible in the website and is not a guarantee about technical logs maintained by hosting or third-party providers. Do not assume that information entered into a browser is suitable for storing regulated or highly confidential data.

6. Your choices

  • Use your browser settings to clear locally stored website data.
  • Export a My Workspace backup before clearing site data or changing devices.
  • Manage downloaded reports through your device's file manager.
  • Review the privacy policies of external providers when their resources are loaded.

7. Children's privacy and policy updates

This website is a general-purpose garment calculation tool and is not designed to collect information from children. Website features and data practices may change; this page may be updated when changes are made. Please review the displayed update date.

This policy is a plain-language description of the website's visible functionality, not legal advice or a certification of legal compliance. Hosting logs and third-party services are governed by their providers' terms and policies.
