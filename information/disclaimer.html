Calculation estimates only

Fabric Consumption provides calculation tools to support garment-related work. Results depend on user-entered measurements, fabric specifications, selected methods, units, size ratios, allowances, wastage, rates and rounding. Actual requirements may differ.

Production variables

Real fabric and material consumption can be affected by marker efficiency, pattern and size changes, fabric width, shrinkage, relaxation, spreading, cutting loss, fabric defects, workmanship, supplier tolerances and factory procedures. Not every production variable can be represented in a calculator.

Verify before taking action

Cross-check results against approved patterns, markers, sample measurements, fabric tests, supplier quotations, buyer requirements and your organization's approved procedures. A generated PDF or Excel report does not mean the figures have been independently validated.

Pricing, costing and commercial decisions

Pricing and FOB outputs depend on the rates, quantities, currency settings and assumptions supplied by the user. Confirm exchange rates, charges, taxes, commissions, freight, duties and commercial terms from the relevant authoritative sources before making a commitment.
