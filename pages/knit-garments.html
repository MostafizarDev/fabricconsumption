Formula Reference
Body Consumption
(Body Length + Sleeve Length) × (½ Chest) × 2 × GSM × Quantity
× Wastage %
1,00,00,000 (CM)    or    15,50,000 (Inch)
Collar Consumption
Collar Length × Collar Width × GSM × Quantity
× Wastage %
1,00,00,000 (CM)    or    15,50,000 (Inch)
Cuff Consumption
Cuff Length × Cuff Width × 2 × GSM
× Wastage %
1,00,00,000 (CM)    or    15,50,000 (Inch)
Pocket Consumption
Pocket Length × Pocket Width × No. of Pockets × GSM × Quantity
× Wastage %
1,00,00,000 (CM)    or    15,50,000 (Inch)
Half-moon Consumption
Half-moon Length × Half-moon Width × GSM
× Wastage %
1,00,00,000 (CM)    or    15,50,000 (Inch)
Quantity = pieces  |  Per Dozen = Per Piece × 12  |  Individual wastage is applied to each component
