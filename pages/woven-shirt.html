Woven Shirt · Yards per Dozen
Length (cm) × Width (cm) × Panels × 12
× (1 + Wastage % ÷ 100)

Woven fabric is calculated by length; GSM is not required. Order yards = yards/dozen × order quantity ÷ 12. Wastage is applied to the total net requirement.
