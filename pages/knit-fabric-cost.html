📐 Formula Reference
1. Total Manufacturing Cost

Fabric Cost + Trims Cost + CM + Accessories

2. Cost with Overhead

Total Manufacturing Cost × (1 + Overhead % ÷ 100)

3. FOB Price

Cost with Overhead × (1 + Profit % ÷ 100)

4. C&F Price

FOB Price + Freight per Piece

5. CIF Price

C&F Price × (1 + Insurance % ÷ 100)

All cost values are calculated per garment piece.
